Mutelist authors

Pubkeys whose published mutelist (kind 10000) is treated as a ban source. Anyone they mute is also banned on this relay — effectively outsourcing moderation to trusted curators. You can list as many authors as you like; the relay folds each one's public mute entries into the blacklist.

Private mute entries live in the event's encrypted .content and need the author's private key to read, so the relay can't pull them. Only the relay owner's own private mutelist can be synced — decrypted in your browser by your signer and pushed to the relay.
